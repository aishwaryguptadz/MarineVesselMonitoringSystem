PART 1 — MARPOL ANNEX VI OVERVIEW

Full Title   : International Convention for the Prevention of
Pollution from Ships — Annex VI
“Regulations for the Prevention of Air Pollution
from Ships”

Adopted      : 27 September 1997 (1997 Protocol to MARPOL)
In Force     : 19 May 2005
Applies To   : All ships 400 GT and above engaged in
international voyages. As of 2023, over 95% of
world shipping tonnage falls under ratified
jurisdictions.

Scope        : Limits NOx, SOx, particulate matter (PM),
ozone-depleting substances (ODS), and greenhouse
gas (GHG) emissions from ships.

Key Chapters :
Chapter 1  — General (Reg 1–9): Definitions, surveys,
certificates, Port State Control
Chapter 2  — Survey & Certification (Reg 5–10):
IAPP Certificate requirements
Chapter 3  — Requirements for Control of Emissions (Reg 12–18):
NOx, SOx, ODS, VOC, incineration
Chapter 4  — Energy Efficiency (Reg 19–28):
EEDI, EEXI, SEEMP, CII

PART 2 — NOx EMISSION REGULATIONS (REGULATION 13)

Authority    : MARPOL Annex VI, Regulation 13
NOx Technical Code 2008 (Resolution 2)
Applies To   : All marine diesel engines > 130 kW installed
on ships constructed on or after 1 Jan 2000.
Excludes emergency engines, lifeboat engines.

-----

## 2.1  NOx TIER LIMITS (g/kWh) — based on rated engine speed n

TIER I   (Ships built 1 Jan 2000 – 31 Dec 2010)
Resolution: MARPOL Annex VI (1997 Protocol)
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  n < 130 rpm          :  17.0 g/kWh                 │
│  130 ≤ n < 2000 rpm   :  45·n^(-0.2) g/kWh         │
│  n ≥ 2000 rpm         :  9.8 g/kWh                  │
│  Typical slow-speed   : ~18.1 g/kWh (real-world avg)│
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

TIER II  (Ships built 1 Jan 2011 onwards — global)
Resolution: MARPOL Annex VI amendments (2008)
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  n < 130 rpm          :  14.4 g/kWh                 │
│  130 ≤ n < 2000 rpm   :  44·n^(-0.23) g/kWh        │
│  n ≥ 2000 rpm         :  7.7 g/kWh                  │
│  Typical slow-speed   : ~10.5–12.5 g/kWh            │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

TIER III (Ships built 1 Jan 2016 onwards — ECA only)
Resolution: MARPOL Annex VI amendments (2008)
Active ECAs: North American, US Caribbean (from 2016)
Baltic Sea, North Sea (from 2021 — MEPC.286(71))
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  n < 130 rpm          :  3.4 g/kWh                  │
│  130 ≤ n < 2000 rpm   :  9·n^(-0.2) g/kWh          │
│  n ≥ 2000 rpm         :  2.0 g/kWh                  │
│  Typical slow-speed   : ~3.4 g/kWh                  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Note: Tier III is ~75–80% reduction vs Tier II.
Technologies used: SCR (Selective Catalytic Reduction),
EGR (Exhaust Gas Recirculation), LNG fuel switching.

Real-World Average (ICCT 2023 study — measured emissions):
Mean NOx : 12.46 g/kWh
Range    : 1.06 – 37.71 g/kWh
(Higher at low engine loads — +15–25% below 50% MCR)

-----

## 2.2  NOx COMPLIANCE IN YOUR AI MODEL

built_year < 2000  → Tier0 (pre-regulation baseline ~18.1)
2000–2010          → Tier I  (~12.5 g/kWh typical)
2011–2015          → Tier II (~10.5 g/kWh typical)

nox_g_kwh < 3.5   → ECA-compliant (Tier III)

PART 3 — SOx EMISSION REGULATIONS (REGULATION 14)

Authority    : MARPOL Annex VI, Regulation 14
IMO 2020 Global Sulphur Cap (in force 1 Jan 2020)

-----

## 3.1  GLOBAL SULPHUR LIMITS (fuel sulphur % m/m)

Period                  Global Limit    ECA Limit
─────────────────────── ─────────────── ───────────
Before 1 Jan 2012       4.50%           1.50%
1 Jan 2012 – 30 Jun 2010 3.50%          1.00%
1 Jul 2010 – 31 Dec 2014 3.50%          1.00%
1 Jan 2015 onwards      3.50%           0.10%
1 Jan 2020 onwards      0.50%  ★ IMO 2020  0.10%

★ IMO 2020 Sulphur Cap: Most impactful environmental
regulation in shipping history. Required switch from
HFO (3.5%) to VLSFO (0.5%) or MGO (0.1%).

-----

## 3.2  SOx EMISSION CALCULATION (used in your dataset)

Formula : SOx (kg/tonne fuel) = 20 × Sulphur%
Source  : EMEP/EEA Air Pollutant Emission Inventory
Guidebook 2021

Fuel Type    Sulphur%    SOx (kg/t fuel)   Status
──────────── ─────────── ───────────────── ──────────────
HFO          2.70%       54.0 kg/t         Non-compliant
(scrubber req.)
VLSFO        0.49%       9.8 kg/t          IMO 2020 compliant
MGO          0.10%       2.0 kg/t          ECA compliant
LNG          ~0.001%     ~0.02 kg/t        Near zero SOx

Scrubber (EGCS) Allowance:
Ships fitted with an approved exhaust gas cleaning
system (scrubber) may continue burning HFO globally.
Open-loop scrubbers reduce SOx by ~98%.
As of 2023: ~35% of large vessels fitted with scrubbers.

ECA Zones (SOx 0.10% limit):
- Baltic Sea (since 2006)
- North Sea (since 2007)
- North American Area (since 2012)
- US Caribbean Sea Area (since 2014)

Dataset columns: fuel_sulphur_pct, sox_kg_per_tonne_fuel,
sox_kg_voyage, sox_g_kwh, scrubber_fitted

PART 4 — CII: CARBON INTENSITY INDICATOR (REGULATION 28)

Authority    : MARPOL Annex VI, Regulation 28
Resolution MEPC.328(76) — adopted June 2021
In force: 1 November 2022
Mandatory from: 1 January 2023

Applies To   : All cargo ships, RoPax, and cruise vessels
of 5,000 GT and above (international trade).
Ships 400–4,999 GT: voluntary / encouraged.

-----

## 4.1  CII FORMULA

Attained CII = CO2_emitted (grams)
─────────────────────────────────────────
DWT (tonnes) × Distance_sailed (nautical miles)

Unit: gCO2 / (DWT·NM)

CO2 Emission Factors (CF) per fuel type:
┌──────────── ────────────┐
│ HFO         3.114 t/t  │
│ VLSFO       3.151 t/t  │
│ MGO/MDO     3.206 t/t  │
│ LNG         2.750 t/t  │
│ LPG (prop.) 3.000 t/t  │
│ Methanol    1.375 t/t  │
└──────────── ────────────┘
Source: MEPC.364(79), Table 1

Required CII = CII_ref × (1 – Z/100)
where:
CII_ref = a × DWT^(–c)   [ship-type specific — see below]
Z       = annual reduction factor (%)

-----

## 4.4  CII RATING BOUNDARIES (A to E)
Source: MEPC.354(78) — Annex 13

The rating is determined by comparing Attained CII
to the Required CII using exponential boundary factors:

Rating  Condition                     Meaning
──────  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
A       Attained < exp(d1) × Required  Major superior
B       exp(d1) ≤ Att < exp(d2) × Req  Minor superior
C       exp(d2) ≤ Att < exp(d3) × Req  Moderate (compliant)
D       exp(d3) ≤ Att < exp(d4) × Req  Minor inferior
E       Attained ≥ exp(d4) × Required  Inferior

Boundary values (d1, d2, d3, d4) are ship-type specific.
General approximation used in models:
d1 = -0.25  →  exp(-0.25) ≈ 0.779
d2 = -0.10  →  exp(-0.10) ≈ 0.905
d3 = +0.10  →  exp(+0.10) ≈ 1.105
d4 = +0.25  →  exp(+0.25) ≈ 1.284

CII Ratio = Attained CII / Required CII
ratio < 0.779  → Rating A
ratio < 0.905  → Rating B
ratio < 1.105  → Rating C
ratio < 1.284  → Rating D
ratio ≥ 1.284  → Rating E

-----

## 4.5  COMPLIANCE CONSEQUENCES

Rating  Consequence
──────  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
A       No action required. Excellent performance.
B       No action required. Minor superior.
C       Compliant. No corrective action needed.
D       If D for 3 consecutive years → corrective plan req.
E       Immediate corrective action plan required.
SEEMP Part III must be updated and verified.
Risk of port bans / higher port dues.

Corrective Action Plan Requirements (D×3 or E):

- Must be submitted to flag Administration or RO
- Must be approved before Statement of Compliance issued
- Includes: CII improvement measures, timeline, targets
- Reporting deadline: by 20 April of the following year

Reporting Timeline:

- Data collection: Calendar year (Jan–Dec)
- Submission to flag / RO: by 31 March of following year
- Verification + Rating assignment: by 30 June
- Statement of Compliance (SOC) issued: after verification

Dataset columns: cii_rating, cii_attained, cii_required,
cii_ratio, is_compliant,
needs_corrective_action, consecutive_d_flag

PART 5 — EEXI: ENERGY EFFICIENCY EXISTING SHIP INDEX

Authority    : MARPOL Annex VI, Regulation 23
Resolution MEPC.328(76) — in force 1 Nov 2022
Mandatory: 1 January 2023 (first EEXI survey)

Applies To   : Existing ships 400 GT and above (all types).
One-time certification (not annual like CII).

Purpose      : Sets a minimum technical energy efficiency
standard for existing ships — equivalent to
EEDI Phase 2 baseline.

Attained EEXI = CO2 / (Capacity × Speed)  [design-based]

If Attained EEXI > Required EEXI:
Ship must implement an Engine Power Limitation (EPL)
or shaft power limitation to reduce power output.

Key difference from CII:
EEXI = design/technical efficiency (one-time)
CII  = operational efficiency (annually measured)

# ================================================================
PART 6 — EEDI: ENERGY EFFICIENCY DESIGN INDEX (NEW SHIPS)

Authority    : MARPOL Annex VI, Regulation 21
In force: 1 January 2013

Applies To   : New ships 400 GT and above
(tankers, bulk carriers, gas carriers, general
cargo, container ships, refrigerated cargo,
combination carriers, ro-ro, cruise).

Phases:
Phase 0 (2013–2014) : Reference baseline
Phase 1 (2015–2019) : 10% reduction from Phase 0
Phase 2 (2020–2024) : 20% reduction from Phase 0
Phase 3 (2025+)     : 30% reduction from Phase 0

The CO2 reduction level for Phase 1 was set at 10%
compared to a reference line from ships built 2000–2010.
Phase 3 mandates 30% reduction for applicable ship types.

PART 7 — GHG STRATEGY & 2050 TARGETS

Authority    : IMO 2023 GHG Strategy
Resolution MEPC (adopted July 2023, MEPC 80)
Previous: IMO 2018 Initial GHG Strategy

2023 IMO GHG Strategy Targets:
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 2030: Carbon intensity reduction ≥ 40% vs 2008      │
│ 2030: 5–10% of shipping energy from zero/near-zero  │
│       GHG fuels (target: 10%)                       │
│ 2040: ~70% GHG reduction vs 2008                    │
│ 2050: Net zero GHG emissions (or close to)          │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

IMO Net-Zero Framework (draft, approved MEPC 83 — 2025):
- Regulation of carbon intensity of fuel used by ships
- Economic pricing mechanisms (carbon levy)
- Draft expected for adoption: October 2026 MEPC 84
- Would become new Chapter 5 of MARPOL Annex VI

Alternative Fuels Pathway:
LNG         → ~20–25% less CO2 vs HFO (well-to-wake)
Methanol    → ~15% less CO2 (green methanol: ~95% less)
Ammonia     → Near-zero CO2 (green ammonia)
Hydrogen    → Zero CO2 (green hydrogen)
Bio-fuels   → Lifecycle dependent (up to 90% reduction)

PART 8 — SEEMP: SHIP ENERGY EFFICIENCY MANAGEMENT PLAN

Authority    : MARPOL Annex VI, Regulation 26
Resolution MEPC.395(82) — adopted Oct 2024
(2024 SEEMP Guidelines — latest)

Applies To   : All ships 400 GT and above.

Structure:
SEEMP Part I  — Energy efficiency management plan
(fuel-saving measures, best practices)
SEEMP Part II — Fuel oil consumption data collection plan
(IMO DCS, measurement methodology)
SEEMP Part III — CII operational carbon intensity plan
(For ships ≥ 5,000 GT only)
Contents required:
• CII calculation methodology
• Required CII for next 3 years
• Implementation plan to achieve CII
• Self-evaluation and improvement plan
Renewal: Every 3 years after 2025.

Reporting:
Annual fuel consumption + CII data → flag / RO
Deadline: 31 March of following year
First CII rating issued: 2024 (for 2023 data)

PART 11 — PORT STATE CONTROL & ENFORCEMENT

Authority    : Procedures for Port State Control 2023
Resolution A.1185(33)
Paris MOU, Tokyo MOU, US Coast Guard

Inspection Trigger Factors:

- CII rating D or E (higher inspection risk)
- Deficiency reports from previous port calls
- Age > 15 years (higher risk factor)
- Flag state performance record

Violation Consequences:

- Detention in port
- Prohibition from entering certain ports
- Financial penalties (flag-state dependent)
- Reputational damage (PSC database published)

For your AI model — ALERT triggers:
If cii_rating == “E”          → CRITICAL compliance alert
If consecutive_d_flag == True → WARNING compliance alert
If nox_g_kwh exceeds tier     → NOx violation alert
If fuel_sulphur_pct > 0.50%
AND scrubber_fitted == 0   → SOx violation alert
If in ECA AND sulphur > 0.10%
AND scrubber_fitted == 0   → CRITICAL SOx ECA alert
